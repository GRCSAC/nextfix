AUTHENTICODE SIGNATURE
----------------------
The installer, and the nextfix.exe it installs, are Authenticode-signed through Microsoft
Trusted Signing. You can confirm the publisher independently of any checksum:

  Get-AuthenticodeSignature .\NextFix-Setup-1.1.0.exe | Format-List Status, SignerCertificate

Expected:

  Status:  Valid
  Subject: CN=GRCSAC, O=GRCSAC, L=Union Beach, S=New Jersey, C=US
  Issuer:  O=Microsoft Corporation, C=US

The subject is the value to check: it is the publisher identity and does not change between
releases. The issuer's common name is deliberately not quoted here: Microsoft Trusted Signing
rotates its intermediate CAs ("Microsoft ID Verified CS EOC CA 03", "... CA 04" and so on)
independently of anything the publisher controls.

Trusted Signing issues short-lived certificates; the signature is timestamped, so it remains
valid after the signing certificate itself expires.
